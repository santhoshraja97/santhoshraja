Every incident teaches something. Most of those lessons end up in a postmortem doc that nobody opens again. **Field Notes** is where I'll keep them instead.

## What I'll write about

- **Incidents and on-call:** what actually helps at 3 a.m., and what only looks good in a runbook.
- **Observability:** dashboards that answer questions instead of just showing graphs.
- **SLOs and error budgets:** how to make them useful to product teams, not just SREs.
- **AI in SRE:** where AIOps genuinely reduces toil, and where it just adds noise.

> Reliability is a feature. Nobody notices it, until they do.
